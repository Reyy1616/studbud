About Us

A curriculum-aligned AI study companion for JTMK students

StudBud is an AI-powered study assistant built specifically for students of the Department of Information and Communication Technology (JTMK) at Politeknik Mukah. It delivers explanations, learning guidance, and real-time help for programming problems that stay aligned with the JTMK syllabus and lecturer-approved materials. Instead of handing over finished answers, StudBud offers hints, step-by-step guidance, and concept explanations so students build their own understanding and problem-solving skills.

Our Mission

Bridge the gap between classroom instruction and independent learning. StudBud gives students a private, judgment-free space to ask questions anytime and anywhere, improving confidence in seeking clarification and enhancing overall learning outcomes.

Why We Built It

Many students hesitate to ask questions in class, and general AI tools often give explanations that clash with what lecturers teach. Waiting for help during lab sessions costs valuable time. StudBud addresses all three with syllabus-accurate, on-demand support.

What StudBud Offers

Curriculum-Aligned Knowledge Base

Integrated with JTMK lecture notes, lab manuals, and coding conventions used at Politeknik Mukah.

Active Learning Engine

Acts as a pedagogical tutor, giving hints, logical steps, and debugging guidance rather than direct answers.

Real-Time Assistance

Reduces waiting time during practical sessions so students can resolve coding difficulties and keep moving.

Academic Integrity First

Designed to guide, not to do the work. It encourages critical thinking instead of copy-paste solutions.
